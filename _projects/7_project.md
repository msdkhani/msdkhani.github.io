## Who reaches specialized care?

In my first-author pancreatic cancer study, we examined 5,847 patients in the Froedtert & Medical College of Wisconsin health system from 2000–2023. We studied how neighborhood socioeconomic conditions relate to specialized care utilization at the ZIP-code level.

Greater neighborhood deprivation was associated with lower utilization after adjustment. These are observational, area-level associations; they should not be interpreted as individual-level causes.

[Read the pancreatic cancer study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0320518) · *PLOS ONE*, 2025.
